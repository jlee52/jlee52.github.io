    Protocols and the leaderboard¶

    Results of different controllers are comparable only when they were recorded the same way. A protocol fixes: - the commanded speeds; - the rollouts per speed and their duration; - the start-up transient left out.

    Protocol Speeds (m/s) Rollouts per speed Duration Left out
    locomotion-speed-v1 0.5–4.3, step 0.2 20 20 s first 10 s
    locomotion-speed-walk-v1 0.5–1.9, step 0.2 20 20 s first 10 s
    locomotion-speed-quick-v1 0.7, 1.1, 1.5 3 15 s first 5 s (development only)

    humanlike.rl.evaluate_policy records a policy under a protocol (RL integration). The protocols are for level ground.

    Checking a recording¶

    humanlike validate my-controller-v0 --protocol locomotion-speed-v1 --json validation.json

    The check confirms: - every commanded speed, with its rollouts (falls count, and are reported); - the duration of the rollouts that did not fall, within one 30 Hz step; - the transient left out; - sampling at the physics rate (≥ 100 Hz); - the recording checks on one rollout at the slowest, middle and fastest speed: ground reaction forces, the body map, joint signs and level ground.

    The leaderboard row¶

    humanlike evaluate my-controller-v0 -o results/
humanlike summarize results/results.json -o row.json

    The row holds the headline number of each analysis against the public human reference, side by side, with its bootstrap interval and data coverage. There is no combined score: the analyses measure different things, and a data set without a signal has no value for the analyses that need it.

    The public leaderboard, with submissions as pull requests and data hosted on the Hugging Face Hub, opens with the publication of the accompanying paper.
